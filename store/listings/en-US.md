# Siphon: Fuel Prices

**Short description:** Compare fuel prices and nearby stations across Portugal and Spain.

## Full description

Find fuel stations and compare current prices across Portugal and Spain with a fast, map-first experience.

Siphon helps you:

- Explore station prices on an interactive map.
- Search and filter by country, city, fuel type, price and distance.
- Save favourite stations for quick access.
- Estimate round-trip driving cost and whether a cheaper station is worth the drive.
- Compare vehicle consumption and EV ownership scenarios.
- Review historical prices and broader market context.
- Personalize colours, icons, styles and your location marker.

Location is optional. When requested, it is used to show nearby stations and calculate road distances through the public OSRM routing service. Siphon has no accounts and keeps favourites, vehicles, preferences and history on your device.

Siphon is free, open source and ad-free in this release.
